Semantic HTML 🎀

Semantic elements clearly describe their meaning in a human reledable way.

They tell both the developer and the browser what the content inside is about.

Common Semantic Html Elements:

Element Meaning
header Top section of a page or section.
nav For navigation links.
main Main content of the document.
section Grouping of content.
article Independent content (like a blog post).
aside For sidebar or related info.
footer Bottom section of a page or section.
figure & figcaption for images with captions.
