CSS Positioning

Static Positioning

This is the default positioning for all elements .

Relative Positioning

Elements are positioned relative to their normal position. You can use the top, right, bottom and left properties to adjust the position of the element.

👽

Absolute Positioning

Elements are positioned relative to their nearest positioned ancestor, If there is no positioned ancestor, they are positioned relative to the initial containing block( usually the viewport) .

🧸
📦

Fixed Positioning

Elements are positioned relative to the viewport and do not move when the page is scrolled .

🗒

Sticky Positioning

Elements are positioned based on the user's scroll position. They toggle between relative and fixed positioning depending on the scroll position.
