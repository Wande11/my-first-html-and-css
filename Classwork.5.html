🔥PART 1: PEOPLE WHO PASSED THROUGH PERSECUTION

Persecution means suffering because of faithfulness to God.

  1. Stephen- Persecuted and Martyred
    2. 📖Acts 6-7

    Stephen was one of the first deacons in the early church. He was:

    • Full of faith
    • Full of the Holy Ghost
    • Bold in preaching Christ
    Acts 6:8(KJV)

    And Stephen, full of faith and power, did great wonders and miracles among the people.

    Religious leaders opposed him and brought false accusations.

    Acts 7

    Stephen preached a powerful sermon about Israel's history and their rejection of God's messengers.

    The people were furious.

    Acts 7:54-58

    They stoned him to death .

    As he was dying:

    Acts 7:59-60 (KJV)

    And they stoned Stephen, calling upon God, and saying, Lord Jesus, receive my spirit.

    And he kneeled down, and cried with a loud voice, Lord Jesus, receive my spirit .

    He forgave his killers .

    Lesson: True faith remains even under deadly Persecution.

  2. Daniel - Persecuted for Prayer
    3.  📖Daniel 6

    Daniel was a faithful government leader in Babylon. Other officials envied him.

    They convinced the king to pass a law that no one could pray to any god except the king for 30 days.

    Daniel continued praying openly .

    Daniel 6:10

    Now when Daniel knew that the writing was signed, he went into his house... and he kneeled upon his knees three times a day, and prayed.

    He was thrown into the lion's den.

    But God delivered him.

    Daniel 6:22(KJV)

    My God hath sent his angel, and hath shut the lion's mouths.

      Lesson: Faithfulness in Persecution brings divine preservation.

    But Saul became
